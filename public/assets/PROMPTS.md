# Brief dan prompt aset Bantuin

Aset digambar langsung sebagai geometri SVG. Prompt adalah brief sumber, bukan klaim penggunaan model gambar.

## Ilustrasi dan doodle

Prompt dasar: Original editorial doodle for Bantuin, a service turning website briefs into planning documents. Controlled hand-drawn rounded ink lines, slight irregularity, simple shapes, generous negative space. Sea & Sun palette: ocean blue #167DAD, ink #173444, pale sky #DFF2FA, sunshine yellow #FFD35A, blue-white paper #F0FBFF. Transparent background. No written words, gradients, 3D, heavy shadows, watermarks, stock characters, or certification badges. Match this asset family's geometry.
